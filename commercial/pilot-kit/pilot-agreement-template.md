# Pilot Agreement (Template)

> Short-form letter agreement for a paid 60-day pilot. Designed to
> be signable in days, not weeks. Customer's full procurement
> process happens **after** the pilot, when both sides have data.

This Pilot Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of
**<Effective Date>** by **latence.ai** ("**Provider**") and
**<Customer Legal Name>** ("**Customer**").

## 1. Pilot

Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable,
non-sublicensable license to install and operate the latence-trace
Groundedness Tracker (the "**Software**") inside Customer's own
infrastructure, for the **Pilot Term** of **60 days** beginning on
the Effective Date, solely for the purpose of evaluating the
Software against the Success Criteria attached as **Schedule A**.

## 2. License Key

Provider will issue a Pilot License JWT scoped to the Pilot Term and
to a maximum of **<X>** QPS. The License Key automatically expires
at the end of the Pilot Term unless extended in writing.

## 3. Fee

Customer shall pay a Pilot Fee of **EUR <amount>** within 30 days of
the Effective Date. The Pilot Fee is creditable against the first
year's annual subscription if Customer signs a Master Subscription
Agreement during or within 30 days after the Pilot Term.

## 4. Customer Obligations

Customer will (a) provide a named technical contact, (b) deploy the
Software in a representative environment, (c) provide Provider with
weekly aggregate metrics during the Pilot Term, and (d) participate
in the day-30 mid-pilot review and the day-60 end-of-pilot review.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's confidential information with
the same degree of care it uses for its own (no less than
reasonable). Customer Data remains Customer's; Provider does not
receive Customer Data unless Customer voluntarily shares it for
support.

## 6. Data

The Software runs inside Customer's perimeter and does not transmit
Customer Data to Provider by default. The Data Processing Addendum
(`commercial/data-processing-addendum.md`) is incorporated by
reference for any Personal Data Customer chooses to process through
the Software during the Pilot.

## 7. Warranties; Disclaimer

The Software is provided **AS IS** for the Pilot Term. Provider
disclaims all warranties, express or implied. Customer acknowledges
that groundedness scoring is statistical and that Customer is
responsible for human review of amber-band outputs.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

Each party's aggregate liability under this Pilot Agreement is
capped at the Pilot Fee. Neither party is liable for indirect,
incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, or punitive damages.

## 9. Term and Termination

This Agreement runs for the Pilot Term and terminates automatically
on its expiration. Either party may terminate earlier on 15 days
written notice. On termination Customer must remove the Software
within 30 days. Sections 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 survive.

## 10. Conversion to MSA

If Customer chooses to continue, the parties will execute the
Master Subscription Agreement (`commercial/master-subscription-agreement.md`)
and a corresponding Order Form. The Pilot Fee credit applies as
defined in Section 3.

## 11. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **[jurisdiction]**. It is
the complete agreement between the parties with respect to the
Pilot, superseding any prior verbal or written discussions.
